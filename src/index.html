Engineering profile / 01

Software should be designed as a system, not a collection of screens.

Mouadh Jaber is a Senior Software Engineer working at the intersection of enterprise development, software architecture and integration. His work spans telecommunications, banking, insurance, transport, pharmaceutical supply and agriculture technology—environments where reliability, traceability and thoughtful change matter.

The engineering approach starts with boundaries: understand the business process, map the data and integration flows, define the right component responsibilities, then build a path from a maintainable codebase to a deployable service. That means working deliberately with architecture, APIs, data, security and operational delivery together.

His practical experience includes C# and .NET backend development; REST and WCF services; identity and two-factor authentication; microservices and messaging; Docker and cloud migration; and delivery practices including TDD, BDD, DevOps and DevSecOps.

Engineering principles / 04

The work behind reliable software.

  1. 01

    Make complexity observable

    Model the domain, document interfaces and make data flows explicit before complexity becomes hidden in code and manual processes.

  2. 02

    Design for change

    Use clear boundaries, testable components and stable contracts so systems can evolve without unnecessary rewrites.

  3. 03

    Deliver with operational awareness

    Development does not end at a successful build. Deployment, security, production support and the people operating the system are engineering concerns.
